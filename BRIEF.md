# Cellove Wellness - Landing Page Build Brief

Derived from `Cellove Website Brief.docx` (33 sections) and rewritten as a build spec.
Source brief content last changed 2026-08-12. This document is the authority for the build.

---

## 1. Positioning

Cellove Wellness is a premium, doctor-led **cellular wellness centre** in Kuala Lumpur.
It is not a stem cell clinic, not a spa, not a supplement brand, not a hospital.

**One sentence:** Cellove combines cell technology, absorption science and scientific data
to help people make more informed decisions about their health.

**Emotional core:** Self Love Begins With Cell Love.

**Philosophy:** Patient First. Science First.

## 2. The hero is the patient, not the treatment

The four pillars are supporting cast, never the headline:

1. Cell Technology
2. Absorption Science
3. Data & Science
4. Doctor-led Care

## 3. Hard constraints (non-negotiable)

### Banned from the page
- Leading with treatments or naming a therapy in the hero
- Any price, price range, or price enquiry CTA
- The words: cure, reverse, guaranteed, 100%, miracle, detox, instant, breakthrough, magic
- Glowing DNA, blue cells, generic laboratory imagery
- Medical stock photography
- Hospital, spa, MLM, or supplement-shop visual language
- Stem cell / exosome / NK cell used as brand identity (explained deeper in-site only)
- A disease catalogue with implied cures

### Regulatory
Malaysian health advertising is governed by the Medicine (Advertisement and Sale) Act 1956
and the Medical Advertisements Board. **Every claim on this page, including the title tag and
meta description, requires medical and legal sign-off before publication.** Copy in this build
is drafted conservatively but is not a substitute for that review.

### Words to own
Cell. Science. Data. Evidence. Absorption. Technology. Doctor-led. Personalised.
Understanding. Prevention. Wellness. Health. Care. Trust.

## 4. Audience

People who value health, will invest in premium care, prefer science over the cheapest option,
want prevention and long-term wellbeing, and want evidence rather than internet health myths.

The page must feel premium and intelligent, never mass-market. Premium comes from quality of
thinking, not luxury decoration. Never communicate "we are expensive."

## 5. Conversion

The objective is **a conversation, not a transaction.**

- Primary CTA: **Start Your Cellove Journey**
- Secondary CTA: **WhatsApp Us**
- WhatsApp persists on mobile throughout the site
- Pre-filled WhatsApp message: "Hi Cellove, I would like to learn more about your approach to cellular wellness."

One label per intent. No third variant of "get in touch" anywhere on the page.

## 7. Visual system

**Type**
- Display: Cormorant Garamond. Justified by the existing wordmark, which is a high-contrast
  classical serif. Used at large scale with tight tracking, never for UI or body.
- UI and body: a precise neutral grotesque. Carries all structure so the serif cannot drift spa.
- No third family. No mono.

**Color** (derived from the logo, not from a generic warm-craft palette)
- Paper, the dominant ground
- Stone, a mid warm neutral for section separation
- Deep neutral, used once as a deliberate full-bleed band
- Rose gold, the single locked accent, from the logo heart
- Gold, reserved for hairline and rule work only

One accent, locked across every section. One radius scale. **Light mode only**, by client
instruction: no dark-mode token set, and `color-scheme: light` pins native controls to light
even for visitors whose OS is set to dark.

**Motion**
Restrained. Entry fade on hero, scroll reveal on section headers, tactile press on CTAs.
Everything collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`. No parallax, no scroll hijack, no marquee.

## 8. Imagery

Abstract warm fields are used for atmospheric backgrounds. This is a deliberate answer to the
brief's ban on medical stock and glowing-DNA imagery.

**The following slots need real photography from the client and ship as labeled placeholders:**
- Doctor portraits (3), 4:5, real practitioners with names, qualifications, registrations
- Clinic interior, 16:9, consultation room or arrival space
- Consultation moment, 3:2, doctor and patient, no procedure visible

## 9. SEO

Target: "Malaysia clinic", "Malaysia wellness centre".
Title and meta description are drafted in the build and **flagged for medical-legal review**.
