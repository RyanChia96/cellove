A supplement promises better immunity. A video claims to reverse ageing. Someone shares a remarkable recovery story.

How do you know what to believe?

At Cellove Wellness, we believe that asking the right questions is just as important as finding answers.

Here are five questions worth asking before accepting a health claim.

## 1. What Evidence Supports It?

A personal story may be interesting, but it cannot prove that a treatment caused an improvement. Look for credible research that directly investigates the claim.

## 2. Was It Tested in Humans?

A substance may produce interesting results in a laboratory or in animals. But human bodies are more complex, and early findings do not guarantee benefits in people.

Ask what has actually been demonstrated in human studies.

## 3. How Was the Research Conducted?

Different studies provide different levels and types of evidence. Well-designed controlled clinical trials can help researchers investigate whether an intervention makes a difference. Systematic reviews examine evidence across multiple studies.

The quality of the research matters more than impressive-sounding terminology.

## 4. What Were the Actual Results?

Did the intervention improve how people felt or functioned, or did it only change a laboratory measurement?

A statistically significant result does not always translate into a meaningful health benefit. Safety and side effects matter too.

## 5. Have Other Researchers Confirmed the Findings?

One positive study rarely settles a scientific question. Independent research can help establish whether findings are consistent and reliable.

Also consider who funded the research and whether any conflicts of interest were disclosed.

## Science Over Hype

These questions are not meant to make us reject every new idea. They help us understand what the evidence supports and what remains uncertain.

The next time you encounter an impressive health claim, pause and ask: *What evidence supports it?*
